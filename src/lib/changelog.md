# Changelog

## 28 November 2026

### Added

- A dedicated Settings page for appearance and language, with this changelog.
- An About page with a Telegram support button and the four legal pages.
- Telegram WebApp theme support: light and dark now follow Telegram's own appearance, and links open
  natively inside the app instead of in a separate browser tab.
- A compact Telegram layout: smaller header and hero, calmer decorations, tighter cards and safe-area
  padding at the bottom.
- Legal page templates for imprint, privacy, terms and withdrawal. The operator details are marked
  as placeholders until the real details are supplied.

### Changed

- The language and theme switches moved out of the site header into Settings.
- The handbook now points to Settings when it mentions the language switch.
- The site respects the system colour scheme in ordinary browsers as before, and the Telegram
  scheme when opened as a Mini App.

## Earlier in November 2026

- Seven regional channels and TGStat figures added to the overview.
- A visual workflow from source to reader, and separate sections for tools, games and bots.
- German and English URLs with automatic browser language detection and a manual switch.
- A bilingual handbook for the posting, community and suggestion bots.
- App icons, canonical URLs and search metadata for both languages.
