## Right of withdrawal

Consumers have a right of withdrawal subject to the following provisions. A consumer is any natural
person who concludes a contract for purposes predominantly unrelated to their commercial or
self-employed professional activity.

All tools currently offered are free of charge, so there is no payment to refund. This notice
applies in case paid services are offered in the future.

## Withdrawal period

You have the right to withdraw from this contract within fourteen days without giving a reason. The
period begins on the day you or a third party named by you conclude the contract.

## Consequences of withdrawal

If you withdraw, we will refund all payments received immediately and at the latest within fourteen
days from receipt of your withdrawal notice. For the refund we use the same payment method you used
for the original transaction, unless you expressly agreed otherwise.

## Early expiry

For contracts concerning digital content, the right of withdrawal expires once we have begun
performance after you expressly agreed that we begin before the withdrawal period ends and you
confirmed that you are aware of losing your right of withdrawal.
